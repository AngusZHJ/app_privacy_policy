Privacy Policy

Developer: LupLdger

Effective November 11, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

LupLdger is provided by LupLdger. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app. Your records are stored on your device. No account is required, and the app does not provide cloud sync.

2. Information on your device

The app stores project names, craft types, row targets and progress, material and pattern notes, activity history, pattern sections, active and saved work sessions, yarn inventory and links, templates, and reference favorites locally so its features work. Text you choose to enter may contain personal information. It is not uploaded to the developer as part of normal app operation. Temporary search filters, selections, and calculator inputs may remain only for the current view or session.

3. No automatic collection or tracking

LupLdger does not display ads, track your use for advertising or analytics, or automatically upload your records. We do not automatically receive your content or usage history, and we do not sell or share it for advertising.

4. How local information is used

Your saved information is used on your device to display and edit records, calculate the results and summaries you request, restore your place, and create backups or exports you choose. Your records are not used for advertising or to build a profile about you.

5. Permissions and selected files

LupLdger does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo-library access. Backup import and export use the system file picker; only the file or destination you select is used. Choosing a cloud file provider may cause that provider to transfer your selected file under its own settings.

6. Sharing, exports, and system services

When you export a backup, it is saved to the destination you choose. If you choose a cloud file provider, that provider may transfer or store the copy under its own privacy practices. Apple may also include app data in device backups or handle diagnostic information according to your system settings. These system services are separate from this app; we do not automatically receive your project records.

7. Retention and deletion

Delete projects, yarn items, templates, or favorites using their in-app controls. Archiving a project does not delete it, and deleting one project does not delete independent inventory or templates. Activity, undo, and session histories have the limits shown in the app. Other local records remain until you remove or replace them, within the app's storage limits. To remove this app's data from your device, choose Delete App in iOS Settings; Offload App can keep documents and data. Manage exported files and device or cloud backups separately. Deleting this app does not remove those copies.

8. Your control over information

You can review and edit local records and export a backup from Settings. There is no online account or cloud copy for us to access. We cannot view, edit, restore, or remotely delete records kept only on your device. You decide whether to export content and which optional system permissions to allow.

9. Security

Your saved records use iOS file protection. Protect your device with appropriate security settings and keep backups private. No storage method can guarantee against data loss or unauthorized access. Exported backup files are not password-protected or encrypted by this app.

10. Children and personal information

The app does not ask for an age, identity profile, or child account, and does not automatically collect records from children. Parents and guardians should help children avoid entering or sharing sensitive personal information. User-entered records stay subject to the same local-storage behavior described above.

11. If you email us

Contact is optional. If you email caoxinmei221@Outlook.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send through our email provider. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and provide support, not to track activity in the app or advertise to you. Please avoid sending private project notes unless needed. Support correspondence is retained only as needed for the request, reasonable follow-up, or legal obligations. You may use the same address to ask about access, correction, or deletion of correspondence, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.

12. Policy updates

This policy is included in the app so it can be read offline. If app behavior or this policy changes, the updated policy will show a new effective date in the app. A policy update alone does not turn on tracking or upload your existing records.
