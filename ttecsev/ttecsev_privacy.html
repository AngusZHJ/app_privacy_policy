Privacy Policy

Developer: TtEcSev

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Last updated: November 11, 2026

How TtEcSev handles the information you keep in your appliance library.

1. Scope & Developer

This policy applies to the TtEcSev iPhone app provided by the developer TtEcSev. The app is an offline tool for appliance records and maintenance. No account, sign-in, or developer-operated cloud service is required.

2. Information You Add

You may enter appliance names, rooms, brands, models, serial numbers, purchase and warranty dates, maintenance plans, completion records, consumable details, repair descriptions, costs, and service provider information. You may also select receipt or warranty photos, manuals, and other supported documents. These items may contain personal information. Add only information you want to keep and are authorized to use.

3. Local Use & Storage

The app processes this information on your iPhone to display your library, search records, calculate maintenance dates, show warranty reminders, preview attachments, and create or restore backups. It saves your library in its local app storage and creates temporary files for attachment previews. TtEcSev does not upload this content to developer servers. The developer cannot remotely view, retrieve, correct, or delete your local library.

4. No Tracking or Advertising

TtEcSev does not include advertising, analytics tools, tracking technology, or a developer-operated data collection service. The app does not sell or rent your information or automatically transmit your library to the developer. Apple may separately process App Store, device backup, or operating-system diagnostic information according to your settings and Apple's policies; this policy describes the app's own behavior.

5. Photos, Files & Notifications

The system photo picker lets you choose individual photos without giving the app access to your entire photo library. The system file picker provides access to the files you select. Imported attachments are copied into the app's library. Notification permission is requested when you save a maintenance plan with a reminder enabled. If allowed, the app gives iOS the task title, appliance name, and reminder time to schedule a local notification. These details may appear on your Lock Screen depending on your notification settings. No remote push-notification service is used.

6. Sharing, Exports & Other Providers

The app does not automatically share your records. If you use a system share action or save an exported backup to Files, the recipient or storage provider you select receives that content. Choosing iCloud Drive or another cloud provider can transfer it under that provider's terms and privacy practices. The developer does not control those services. Full backups include the library and attachment contents in an unencrypted JSON file; store and share them carefully.

7. Retention & Deletion

Records remain in the active local library until you delete them, replace the library by restoring a backup, or remove the app and its data. Tap the trash icon beside an attachment to delete its app copy. Deleting an appliance also removes its associated plans, attachments, consumables, and history. The app clears its corresponding temporary preview copies when attachments are deleted. Original photos and files outside the app are not deleted. Previously exported backups and copies held by recipients are not updated or deleted automatically. Manage those copies in the relevant storage location. Offloading the app may retain data; use iOS Delete App if you intend to remove the installation and its local data. Device and iCloud backups are managed separately through iOS.

8. Your Controls & Security

You can review and edit records in the app, export the library, and delete local content without contacting the developer. Disable a plan's reminder or revoke notification permission in iPhone Settings to stop optional reminders. No account deletion request is needed because the app creates no account. App storage uses iOS file protection, but no storage method guarantees complete security. Protect your iPhone with a passcode and keep a separate backup before deleting data, restoring a library, replacing your device, or uninstalling the app.

9. Children & Sensitive Information

TtEcSev is a general household organizer and is not designed specifically for children. It does not request age information or provide an online collection service for children's information. Avoid storing unnecessary sensitive information in receipts, notes, or attachments. If you share a device, consider who can open the app or see reminder previews.

10. Policy Changes

We will update this policy when our privacy practices change and show the latest revision date above. We will notify you of material changes as required by applicable law and ask for any permissions or consent required before using your information in a new way. You can review the current policy in Settings at any time.

11. Contact & Support Correspondence

The developer responsible for this app is TtEcSev. For support or privacy questions, email Rukminikurup@icloud.com using your preferred email app. The app does not send inquiries automatically. If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you include. We use that information to respond to your inquiry and resolve the issue, and retain it only as long as needed for that purpose or as required by applicable law. Correspondence is handled through our email provider and is not used for advertising. You may use the same address to ask about or request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations. Do not send sensitive receipts or other personal information unless needed for your inquiry. The developer cannot remotely access or delete your local library; you can manage it directly in the app and in iPhone Settings.
