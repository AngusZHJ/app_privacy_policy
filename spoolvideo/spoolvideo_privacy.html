Privacy Policy

Developer: SpoolVideo

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Your accounts, imported videos, watch history, favorites, and coin balances are stored on this iPhone. SpoolVideo does not upload this information or collect data for analytics or advertising.

You choose the videos to import from Files or your photo library. Apple or your file provider may download videos stored in the cloud.

Deleting an account removes its videos and records from SpoolVideo. Uninstalling the app or deleting its data may permanently remove your coins and unlocked features. Your accounts, coin balances, check-in records, daily task progress, and unlocked features don't sync across devices.

Coins are earned for free and used only to unlock features in SpoolVideo.

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple handles identity authorization, which requires a connection to Apple. We keep a protected account identifier and an optional display name on this iPhone. We do not request your email address or receive your Apple password. Apple sign-in does not restore your videos, coins, Pro, or other unlocks after data loss or a device change. Deleting your profile removes its local data and credentials. To also disconnect Apple sign-in, manage SpoolVideo under Sign in with Apple in iPhone Settings.
