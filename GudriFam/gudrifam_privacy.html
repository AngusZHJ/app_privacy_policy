Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to GudriFam, an offline moving organizer developed by GudriFam. GudriFam does not collect your app content on a developer-operated server or send it to the developer. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking, and does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Data on Your Device

Move names, dates, rooms, boxes, items, notes, and selected photos are stored in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. This data may be included in device backups managed by your iOS settings.

The system photo picker lets you select individual photos. GudriFam receives only the photos you choose and stores resized copies. It does not request access to your entire photo library.

Exports and Your Choices

Backup files contain your app data and photos. Labels contain box details. When you export, share, or print through a system service, that service receives the content you choose. System device backups may also include app data, depending on your iOS settings. Your chosen services’ privacy practices apply.

GudriFam does not sell your personal information. Exported backup files are not encrypted by the app. Keep them in a location you trust and share them only with people or services you choose.

You can edit or delete individual records, or permanently remove all app data with Delete All Data in Settings. Exported files and device backups must be managed separately.

Retention and Contact Requests

Records stay in the app’s private storage until you delete them, replace them by restoring a backup, or remove the app and its data. Because the developer does not receive your local records, the developer cannot access, recover, or delete them remotely.

If you contact the developer by email, you choose what to include in your message. Do not send passwords or sensitive inventory details. Your email service’s privacy practices apply to messages you send.

Policy Updates

If this policy changes, its effective date and updated text will appear on this page with the applicable app update. No internet connection is needed to read this policy or use GudriFam’s organizing features.
